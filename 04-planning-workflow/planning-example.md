# Planning Workflow Example

## Goal

Plan a four-week Business Analysis initiative to assess and improve the fictional employee service request process, with an agreed future process, prioritised requirements, identified risks, and a clear recommendation for the next stage.

## Success Measures

- The current service request process is documented and key pain points are identified.
- Business and user requirements are gathered and prioritised.
- Security, access, and process dependencies are identified.
- Relevant stakeholders review the proposed future process.
- A human decision-maker approves or rejects the recommended next step.

## Mechanisms

The plan will be delivered through five mechanisms:

1. **Process analysis:** document the current process, roles, pain points, and dependencies.
2. **Requirements management:** gather, clarify, and prioritise business and user requirements.
3. **Stakeholder alignment:** review findings and proposed changes with relevant stakeholders.
4. **Risk and dependency tracking:** record unresolved issues, ownership gaps, and dependencies.
5. **Decision support:** prepare a concise recommendation for the next stage.

## Phases and Tasks

| Phase | Purpose | Actionable Tasks | Main Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Understand the Current Process | Establish a clear view of how requests are currently handled | Review stakeholder notes; map the current process; identify roles, manual steps, pain points, and dependencies | Current-state process map and findings |
| 2. Gather Requirements | Define what users and the business need from an improved process | Conduct stakeholder discussions; document requirements; identify assumptions and open questions; clarify security needs | Requirements list and clarification log |
| 3. Prioritise and Design | Identify the most important improvements and define a possible future process | Prioritise requirements; map the proposed future process; compare improvement options; identify risks and dependencies | Prioritised requirements and proposed future process |
| 4. Review and Recommend | Support stakeholders in deciding the next step | Prepare a decision-ready summary; review findings with stakeholders; document feedback; present recommendation and open decisions | Stakeholder-reviewed recommendation |
| 5. Finalise | Record the agreed outcome and next steps | Update documentation based on approved feedback; record decisions; prepare the final action list | Approved next-step plan |

## Task Sequence

1. Review the existing stakeholder information.
2. Document the current employee service request process.
3. Identify the main pain points, risks, and dependencies.
4. Gather and clarify business and user requirements.
5. Identify security and access-control requirements.
6. Prioritise the requirements based on business and user impact.
7. Develop and review a proposed future process.
8. Compare potential improvement options.
9. Present the findings and recommendation to the relevant stakeholders.
10. Record the human decision and agreed next steps.

## Dependencies and Open Decisions

- The availability of relevant stakeholders may affect the requirements-gathering stage.
- Security and access requirements require input from the appropriate specialists.
- The final future process depends on stakeholder review and approval.
- The decision to introduce a central service portal has not yet been confirmed.
- The decision on whether the portal would replace email completely remains open.
- Exact owners and deadlines for some activities require stakeholder confirmation.

## Human Decision Points

- A human stakeholder reviews and validates the documented current process.
- Relevant business and technical stakeholders confirm the requirements.
- Appropriate specialists review security and access requirements.
- Stakeholders review and approve the proposed future process.
- The authorised decision-maker makes the final decision on the recommended next step.

AI can support the planning and documentation process, but final priorities, requirements, risks, and decisions remain subject to human review and approval.
